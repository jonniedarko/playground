The application notes and parts datasheets each work through a complete
reference circuit: AN650's light controller, the DX300's stepper motor
example, and the 100P-14's packet reverser. Every one of those pages already
states what the circuit must do, in the manual's own words, before showing
the finished wiring.

The pages in this section pull that brief out on its own - the requirement,
the parts allowed, and a table of exactly what a correct circuit is expected
to produce - so it can be read and attempted before looking at the answer.
The finished reference circuit still lives on its original datasheet or
application note page, linked from each brief below.
