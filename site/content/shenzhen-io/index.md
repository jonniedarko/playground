SHENZHEN I/O ships with a manual meant to be printed and put in a three-ring
binder: a language reference for the MCxxxx microcontroller family, datasheets
for every part you can drop on a circuit board, application notes from 诚尚Micro,
and a pile of supplemental documents that turn up as puzzle specifications.

These pages are a transcription of that binder, reorganised for reading on a
screen. The section layout follows the original tab dividers.

## The five tabs

The printed binder is divided into five tabbed sections:

1. **Application Notes** - short design guides from the chip vendor.
2. **Language Reference** - the MCxxxx instruction set and program model.
3. **Parts Datasheets** - one sheet per component.
4. **Supplemental Data** - the documents individual puzzles hand you.
5. **Engineering Notes** - blank paper. Bring your own.

## Where to start

**New to it?** Read the [quick start](/shenzhen-io/quick-start/). It is one page:
