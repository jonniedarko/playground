Parts datasheets

One page per component - microcontrollers, memory, logic, displays, sensors and everything else you can place on a board.

Every part you can place on a circuit board has a datasheet in the binder. The pages below group them by what they do.

The components this site can draw: the MC4000, MC4000X and MC6000 microcontrollers, the DX300 expander, 100P-14 RAM, 200P-14 ROM, a generic I/O terminal, and the four LC70Gxx logic gates.
